NEUROSPACE™ | XR OR SPACE

The Mixed Reality Operating Room.
Where Digital Intelligence Enters the Surgical Field.

The operating room is where information becomes action.

The Neurospace XR OR Space brings imaging, planning, collaboration, and education directly into the surgical environment through Mixed Reality and spatial computing.

Instead of looking away from the patient toward separate monitors, surgeons can access critical information within their field of view, creating an assisted surgical experience where digital intelligence complements physical anatomy in real time.

From image-guided procedures and spatial visualization to remote collaboration and surgical education, XR OR transforms the operating room into a connected, interactive workspace built around the surgeon.

  • Mixed Reality-assisted surgical visualization directly within the surgeon’s field of view
  • Real-time access to imaging, anatomy, trajectories, targets, and procedural information
  • Spatial visualization of patient-specific anatomy within the operating environment
  • Seamless transition from pre-operative planning to intraoperative visualization
  • Remote surgical collaboration and expert guidance through Neurospace Teleportal
  • Collaborative remote consultation and surgical planning
  • Real-time spatial annotation and shared visualization across locations
  • Immersive surgical education, observation, recording, and replay
  • Integration with Neurospace planning tools and existing surgical technologies
  • Connects surgeons, specialists, trainees, and institutions within one Mixed Reality environment
